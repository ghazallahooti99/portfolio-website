Hi, I'm Ghazal

Architectural & Urban Designer · Design Researcher · Climate & Human-Centred Designer

Architectural and urban designer/researcher working at the intersection of environmental performance, human-centred research, and computational design. Grounded in an MSc in Sustainable Urban Design and a background in Architectural Engineering, I approach design as an iterative, evidence-based process: Research → Understand Context & People → Design → Evaluate → Iterate.

Rather than treating environmental analysis as an isolated post-design audit, I embed microclimate simulation, GIS spatial analytics, and qualitative user research directly into early-stage concept development to shape responsive spatial strategies. I am driven by R&D workflows that leverage scenario modelling, process automation, digital twins, and applied AI to connect rigorous inquiry with actionable, high-performance design.

MATERIALITY, CRAFT & FORM

My design process doesn't end at the screen. Whether shaping physical scale models, studying spatial light through photography, or experimenting with structural textures in baking, tangible craftsmanship informs how I understand scale, material limits, and sensory experience.

Translating computational geometry into laser-cut timber, cast plaster, and acrylic. Exploring structural assemblies, tactile connections, and how physical light interacts with solid volumes.

Observing urban life, continuous line structures, and raw material weathering. Photography trains my eye for contrast, pedestrian scale, and atmosphere in built environments.
